## Real Personal Example (used to design Category 6)

Applied to company "X" via an official national government internship portal, received a shortlist email within 15 minutes. A LinkedIn post from person "Y" later described the same company's pattern: unpaid trial/performance-based pay, real work assigned, deadline met, then company went unresponsive to calls and emails after delivery. Key insight: a trusted/government portal listing does not guarantee the employer's actual hiring practices are fair.
